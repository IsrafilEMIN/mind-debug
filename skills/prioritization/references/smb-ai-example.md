# Anchor: focus on applied-AI delivery, not adjacent pursuits

This is an illustrative interview and allocation analysis, not a market claim. Do not assume its answers apply to the user. The v0.1.0 shortcut from “SMB AI” to “do interviews” is not the default procedure.

## First establish the frame

A user says: “I want to scale applied AI for SMBs. I am studying evals, paying for model subscriptions, considering an applied-engineering course, tuning local inference, and building a side project.”

Do not rank these immediately or put them all in sequence. Ask in rounds:

1. What does scaling mean: customer count, reliable delivery capacity, reusable implementation, income, or something else? Why this pursuit, and what would count as a meaningful result?
2. When trade-offs arise, which objective governs? What reliability and risk minimums must be preserved? Is reducing inference cost actually a current constraint?
3. What customers, workflow evidence, delivery experience, tools, representative data, access, time, and budget already exist? What can be implemented now?
4. What currently fails or remains unfinished? What did the last attempt reveal? Where are hours and subscription dollars going, and what outputs do they produce?

Reflect back the resulting frame and obtain confirmation. Then examine the blocker together. Do not assume lack of demand when a customer already exists, or lack of skill when the real problem is unwillingness to complete difficult delivery work.

## Example after confirmation

Suppose the user confirms: the purpose is reliable applied-AI delivery capacity; there is an accessible validated workflow; current representative evaluations expose unresolved failure cases; operating cost is affordable and not the constraint. The user needs to improve and verify the target system, not become an inference-engine researcher.

**Shared bottleneck:** inability to meet the workflow's agreed quality threshold under representative conditions. The evidence is the failing evaluation cases, not the agent's inference from the words “applied AI.”

**Primary effort:** improve and verify this workflow against the agreed threshold, using failure analysis to choose the first implementation change. Define the actual timebox and artifact from the user's capacity; do not invent them here.

| Allocation | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation study and representative tests on this workflow | Attack / Support | Directly diagnoses and improves the current quality constraint. |
| Applied-engineering course | Conditional Support | Keep only relevant material used in this effort with an immediate practice artifact; enrollment alone is not progress. |
| Model/API subscription | Conditional Support | Keep if needed for implementation/evaluation within the agreed budget; review redundant subscriptions rather than defending all spending. |
| Learning through changes to this workflow or a representative task | Support | Practice develops the capability currently required. |
| Local inference tuning for hypothetical future savings | Stop | Optimizes a nonbinding objective and pursues an adjacent discipline. Do not relabel it a later phase of this commitment. |
| Unrelated side project justified as practice | Stop | Learning occurs on a different path; redirect the practice to target-work requirements. |

Focus does not mean “cancel every subscription and stop learning.” It means resources converge on the same current constraint. Several coordinated inputs can be aligned; one apparently inexpensive hobby project can still be a major attention diversion.

## Reversals require evidence, not convenient rationalizations

- If a signed offline requirement actually blocks the current delivery, local-runtime feasibility may become the primary effort. Test an existing supported runtime before assuming kernel work is necessary.
- If measured inference costs prevent sustainable operation now, cost work may be justified. Ask for the actual economics and compare it against the primary objective; do not elevate speculative savings over delivery.
- If the purpose is inference research or personal mastery instead, confirm that different frame. Kernel work may be aligned. Do not impose SMB revenue as the goal.
- If no problem, access, or demand is established, the interview must establish what is missing and what evidence exists before selecting discovery work. “Do customer interviews” is not a universal answer.

## Protect quality without changing direction

Diagnose whether the workflow needs conventional software, deterministic automation, AI, human judgment, or no change. That is a design check inside the agreed pursuit, not an invitation to launch multiple business paths. If AI is an explicit business constraint, acknowledge when a workflow is a poor fit instead of forcing AI into it.

Relevant production requirements include representative evaluations, permissions and data handling, bounded actions, failure/timeout behavior, human escalation, observability, recovery, and a responsible operator. Preserve the risk-proportionate minimums; do not promise bug-free delivery or use reliability as a reason to build unrelated infrastructure.
